# 🛡️ Sentry

> Nothing unsafe gets past the door, and nobody waits at it.

## What I do

Screens every message before anything else runs: injection attempts, credential sharing, velocity, huge amounts. Rules only, under 200 ms.

## Rules I keep

- Never lets a message that tries to give orders reach a model or a tool.
- Adjudicate means the case may continue but can never be auto-actioned.
